  Star topology interpretation:
  Hub-and-spoke creates an asymmetric migration pattern:
  - Hub island receives from ALL others (over-mixing at center)
  - Spoke islands receive ONLY from hub (under-mixing at periphery)
  - Net effect: diversity preserved in spokes, but convergence slowed
  - This is a STRUCTURAL anomaly: λ₂ measures algebraic connectivity
    but doesn't capture the ASYMMETRY of information flow.

    Barbell=0.9786 vs Hypercube=0.9789, d=-0.183
    Barbell diversity=0.0437 vs Disconnected diversity=0.0643

  Barbell interpretation:
  Two 4-island cliques with a single bridge.
  Within-clique: COMPLETE connectivity (λ₂=4 for K4)
  Between-clique: single bridge (bottleneck)
  Net effect: fast mixing within each clique + selective transfer between cliques
  This is the GOLDILOCKS topology — λ₂ underestimates its effective connectivity
  because the INTRA-clique connectivity is what drives early convergence,
  while the bridge preserves inter-clique diversity.
