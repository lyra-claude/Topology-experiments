# ADDITIVE-FIT — Does the LEAF floor contrast = Effect A + Effect B (no interaction)?

Generated by `experiments/analyze_additive_fit.py`. Answers MECHANISM-A.md item 3.

## Estimands (do not chain)

Per-seed matched contrasts (hub_out − hub_in), n=30/K, seeds share landscape + gen-0 pops:
- **Δfloor** = leafWithinDiv@500 contrast — the TARGET (washes to null at K≥2).
- **Δdecay** = leaf decay-slope contrast — Effect A carrier, **DIRECTLY MEASURED** (leaf observable).
- **Δhub**   = hubWithinDiv@500 contrast — Effect B carrier, measured on the HUB; its
  projection onto the LEAF floor is **INFERRED** (only identifiable as a fitted coefficient).

## Test

Additive: Δfloor ~ 1 + Δdecay + Δhub.  Interaction adds γ·(Δdecay·Δhub).
Regressors standardized. Two non-parametric checks (no seed-averaging):
1. **Permutation** (10000 shuffles) on Spearman ρ between additive residuals and the
   interaction regressor. Significant ρ ⇒ additive model leaves interaction structure.
2. **Jackknife (LOO)** out-of-sample SSE, additive vs interaction; Wilcoxon signed-rank
   on per-seed squared-error differences (paired, non-parametric).

## Verdict

NO A×B interaction that IMPROVES the fit at any K. All permutation tests are null
(p_perm ≥ 0.38), and where the Wilcoxon rejects (K=4) it rejects toward ADDITIVITY
(interaction predicts 26/30 held-out seeds worse, γ≈0.008).
The additive superposition Δfloor ≈ βA·Δdecay + βB·Δhub is CONSISTENT with the data.

Caveat: 'consistent with' is a failure-to-reject, not a proof of additivity. With
n=30/K and standardized regressors, power to detect a moderate interaction is limited.

## MEASURED vs INFERRED (Claudius's lock depends on this)

- **Effect A (Δdecay): DIRECTLY MEASURED.** Leaf decay-slope is an independently
  observable leaf quantity; its contrast is significant and same-signed at every K
  (ANALYSIS.md: δ = −0.66/−0.90/−0.89/−0.99). It enters the fit as a measured regressor.
- **Effect B's contribution to the LEAF floor: INFERRED.** Δhub is directly measured on
  the HUB, but the FLOOR is a LEAF quantity. How much of the leaf-floor contrast is
  attributable to B is identified ONLY as the fitted coefficient βB — it is not an
  independently observable leaf quantity. The additive fit UPGRADES the 'A+B cancellation'
  narrative from pure interpretation to a coefficient-level decomposition, but the B→leaf
  channel remains a residual/inferred term, not a direct measurement.

## Power / resolution caveat

Checkpoints are every 10 gens; peak-latency and early decay are under-resolved (peak_gen
clusters at 10–13). n=30/K limits interaction power. The floor δ itself is null at K=2,4
(p=0.56, 0.31), so the TARGET variance is small there — the fit is most informative at
K=0,1 where Δfloor carries signal, and weakest exactly where cancellation is claimed.
