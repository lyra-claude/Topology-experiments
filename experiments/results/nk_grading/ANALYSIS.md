Generated by `experiments/nk_grading_analysis.py`.

## Method

Per-seed metrics computed using EXACT definitions from `stats_diverse_seed.py`:
- **floor**: `leafWithinDiv` at gen 500.
- **peak_gen**: generation of maximum `leafWithinDiv` per seed.
- **decay_slope**: OLS slope of `leafWithinDiv` from peak_gen to gen 500.
- **hub_floor**: `hubWithinDiv` at gen 500.

Mann-Whitney two-sided, n=30 per condition.

**Sign convention**: δ = (#(a>b) − #(a<b)) / (n_a × n_b), a=hub_out, b=hub_in.
Positive δ = hub_out value LARGER than hub_in. For decay_slope (negative = decay),
a NEGATIVE δ means hub_out decays FASTER than hub_in.
